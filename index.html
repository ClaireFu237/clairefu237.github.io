Biomedical Literature Retrieval

Johns Hopkins University · NLP Team Project · Jan–Apr 2026

Built a dense retrieval pipeline that ranks PubMed articles for biomedical questions from BioASQ Task 13b. We fine-tuned a SciBERT bi-encoder on question–article pairs, trained it with hard negatives, and reranked candidates by combining semantic similarity with keyword overlap.

Results: Retrieval fine-tuning raised Hit@1 from 9% to 84% on the original corpus. After adding 1,000 distractor documents, the fine-tuned baseline achieved 82% Hit@1. On that same augmented corpus, hard-negative training and reranking reached 92% Hit@1 and 0.9342 MRR@20.
